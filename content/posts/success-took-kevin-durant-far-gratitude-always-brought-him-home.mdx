Winter hits differently when the kitchen cabinets are bare.

Before the NBA arenas and the endorsement deals, November in Seat Pleasant was not about holiday travel.

It was about figuring out how to put a warm meal on the table when the temperature dropped.

Wanda Durant worked through the night, yet there were still winter evenings when a single paycheck could not stretch far enough to fill the refrigerator.

In those quiet, freezing months, it was the neighborhood that stepped in.

A shared plate of food from a neighbor. A donated holiday box from the local pantry. People with very little of their own making sure Wanda and her boys did not go to sleep hungry.

### The sound of an empty stomach.

Hunger leaves a memory that wealth cannot erase.

You never forget the feeling of opening a refrigerator door and seeing only the light bulb shining back at you.

You never forget the quiet relief when someone knocks on the door holding a bag of groceries.

Kevin Durant grew into a global superstar. He earned enough money to eat at the finest restaurants in any city in the world.

Success could have easily distanced him from those old, uncomfortable memories.

Instead, it pulled him closer to them.

![Gratitude often begins with remembering who fed you when you had nothing.](/images/family/kd-mother-neighborhood.jpg)
*Gratitude often begins with remembering who fed you when you had nothing.*

### Heavy cardboard boxes in the cold.

Every year when the cold wind returns to Prince George's County, Kevin and Wanda return to the pavement where they once stood.

There is no red carpet.

Just stacks of heavy brown cardboard boxes lined up in the chill air.

Inside those boxes are frozen turkeys, canned vegetables, bags of stuffing, and fresh produce.

Wearing thick winter coats, Wanda and Kevin stand for hours handing out food, box by box, family by family.

They are not handing out souvenirs. They are handing out dinner.

![Giving back is sometimes another way of saying, 'We haven't forgotten.'](/images/family/kd-giving-back.jpg)
*Giving back is sometimes another way of saying, 'We haven't forgotten.'*

### Understanding the weight of the box.

When a stranger hands you charity, it can sometimes feel cold.

When Wanda and Kevin lift a heavy turkey box into a mother's arms, there is no distance between them.

They know exactly how heavy that box is.

They know the quiet relief of a parent realizing their children will have a hot meal on the table tonight.

They do not look at the people in line as strangers in need. They see the exact faces that once kept them going through the winter.

![Success feels different when it is shared with the people who believed before the world did.](/images/family/kd-community-smiles.jpg)
*Success feels different when it is shared with the people who believed before the world did.*

---

### No kitchen left cold.

Some athletes use wealth to build higher walls between their past and their present.

Kevin Durant used his success to make sure the kitchen tables in his old neighborhood stay full.

Every turkey handed across that table is a quiet thank you to the streets that fed him before the world even knew his name.

Success took him around the globe.

Remembering the cold always brought him back home.
